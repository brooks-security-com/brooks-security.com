Realtime endpoint management and vulnerability management were the categories I spent the most time on at Syxsense, both in front of prospects and inside the product. The webinar was one part of that job. The rest was proofs of concept, architecture sessions, and RFI and RFP responses for customers in critical infrastructure and government, where the scanning and remediation story has to hold up against a regulator as well as a security team.

## What it covers

The management console as a working surface rather than a feature tour, and what realtime actually means in practice. An agent that reports back on its own schedule, and a console that can act on what it reports without waiting for the next scan window.

## Worth watching

The part where the console stops being a reporting tool. Seeing the state of an estate is the easy half. Being able to change that state from the same place, on the same day, is what separates this from a dashboard.
