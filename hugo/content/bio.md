I am a security and systems engineer, ten years into a career in IT and six of them in security,
and the throughline is that I end up owning what I build: I write the Terraform, I run the log
pipeline, and I am in the room when the auditor asks what happens when it fails. I started on the
operations side at Schweitzer Engineering Laboratories, standardizing the Ubuntu build the company
ran on and hardening Linux fleets to CIS benchmarks. Three years at Syxsense went into designing
and deploying security automation inside other people's environments, and I finished there as
Lead Solutions Architect with a team I had hired and trained behind me. Since March 2024 I have
been senior security and systems engineer at AvatarFleet, where I built the security program from
nothing, took it through SOC 2 Type 1 and Type 2 with zero adverse findings, wrote the Terraform
and Ansible that enforce the controls, and built a SOAR that runs log-based inference against WAF
and ALB logs to shut down adversarial campaigns. Alongside that I run Brooks Security, an
independent vCISO practice. I hold a CISSP, an AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate, an
RHCE, and CompTIA Security+.

I live in Moscow, Idaho with my wife and our son. Family comes first, and that is a constraint
rather than a preference: I left a job once rather than do wrong by the people who worked for me,
and there is no job I would turn down if feeding my family required it. I travel as much as the
calendar allows, and the places I aim for tend to be old. I have read Roman history since I was
twelve, so standing inside it is worth the flight. I train as well, and powerlifting is the habit
I have kept longest, because the bar does not care how I feel about it that morning. Long before
that I played hockey goalie for nine years.
